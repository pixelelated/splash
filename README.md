# Rasteratops splash

Framebuffer splash renderer forked from [ROCKNIX/rocknix-splash](https://github.com/ROCKNIX/rocknix-splash).
The renderer and executable name are retained; the upstream logo is replaced
with Rasteratops's interim wordmark, without an icon (distribution #337).

`tools/rasteratops-wordmark` regenerates the SVG and C data from the unmodified
Tiny5 Duo font. Its authors and SIL Open Font License1.1 are preserved in
`fonts/OFL.txt`. Build with `make`; test in the distribution's VM.

Rasteratops and its artwork follow the distribution's `TRADEMARK.md` and
branding terms. The font remains under its own licence, and the inherited
renderer retains its upstream ownership and licensing.
